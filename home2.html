Week of March 16, 2026
The Cost of Comfort
📖 Joshua 1:9  |  Proverbs 27:17
It's easy to cruise. Show up to work, come home, repeat. But God didn't call us to be comfortable — He called us to be courageous. This week we're digging into what it looks like to step out of autopilot and actually lead in the areas that matter: your home, your faith, your relationships.

Discussion Questions

  1. Where in your life are you playing it safe right now? What would courage look like there?
  2. What's one area where you need another man to call you up, not just comfort you?
  3. Read Joshua 1:9 — what does "the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go" change about how you approach this week?
